CITY WITH ACTIVITY

CROPPED VERSION: the city was trimmed to the area around the 4 vehicles and 4 pedestrians so they are easy to see when zoomed in. Animation is unchanged.

Added 4 low-poly vehicles and 4 articulated pedestrians.

ANIMATED: Import scene.gltf into Blender (File > Import > glTF 2.0). Press Space in the timeline. The animation lasts 12 seconds. Set the playback range to the imported clip to loop. Keep scene.bin, activity.bin and textures beside scene.gltf. Vehicles move back and forth and wheels rotate; pedestrians move and swing their arms and legs.

STATIC PLY: city_with_vehicles_people.ply is frame zero with vertex colors. PLY does not store animation, joints, or texture images. Textures are approximated using vertex colors, so use glTF for the original visual quality. Original line-only outlines are preserved in glTF, omitted in the triangle-mesh PLY.

The original city has not been redesigned. New figures use simple stylized geometry and fixed routes, not autonomous AI navigation.
